1Noun Stacking

Nouns can be combined into longer nouns, similar to German.

Examples
iopfabkey door

2Direct Address

When directly addressing someone, add oa to the word. If the word ends in oa, remove that ending.

Examples
su → suoa

3Default Word Order

The default order is: Subject → Verb → Object → Preposition

The subject comes first, followed by the verb. Objects and other nouns follow the verb. Prepositions come after the subject or as needed.

Examples
Ko proakier bi ZigI program in Zig. (Subject → Verb → Preposition → Language)
Ko makaf Python proakierI made a Python program. (Subject → Verb → Object → Noun)
Ko nak uportI go to school. (Subject → Verb → Object)

4Present Progressive

Add ti as a suffix to a verb to indicate that an action is currently in progress. This is similar to the English -ing ending.

When a verb is stacked with another verb, the ti suffix goes at the very end of the entire construction.

Examples
proakierprogram
proakiertiprogramming
Ko xy proakiertiI am programming.
Ko nakproakiertiI am going programming. (stacked verb with progressive at end)

5Prepositions

Prepositions go after the subject.

Examples
Ko proakier bi ZigI program in Zig.

6Modal Verbs

Modal verbs such as kup (will) and zx (should) go before the verb.

Examples
Ko kup nak uportI will go to school.
Ko zx nak uportI should go to school.
Ko kup proakierI will program.

7Combining Future Tense and Present Progressive

The ti suffix remains attached to the verb when modal verbs are used.

Examples
Ko kup proakiertiI will be programming.

8Negation

The negation word ptu goes before the subject to negate a sentence.

Examples
Ko nak uportI go to school.
ptu ko nak uportI do not go to school.
ptu ko kup nak uportI will not go to school.

9Verb Stacking

Verbs can attach to other verbs, but a verb can have a maximum of one attached verb. That means you can combine two verbs into one construction, but not stack three verbs together.

The attached verb goes after the main verb. The attached verb can describe what the main verb is leading into.

Examples
nakproakiergo programming

10Sentence Modifiers

Words that modify the truth or certainty of an entire sentence go before the subject.

Examples
ptu ko nak uportI do not go to school.
guqi ko proakier bi C++Maybe I program in C++.

11Future and Past Tense

There are many ways to say something in the future or past tense. The primary 3 ways are:

  • Suffix sa → for future tense
  • Suffix af → for past tense
  • Using a word for future/past tense like pu

Note: Past tense (af) cannot be combined with progressive (ti). You must choose one or the other.

Examples
Ko proakiersaI will program.
Ko proakierafI programmed.
Ko pu proakierI have programmed.
Ko proakiertiI am programming (present progressive, not past).

12Temporal Expressions

Time expressions go after the verb.

Examples
Ko nak uport amkaI go to school during the day.
Ko nak uport amkalapI go to school tomorrow.

13Numbers in Sentences

Numbers can be expressed using either numerals or words. When using words, follow the number vocabulary.

When a specific number is used, the noun stays in singular form. Combining a number with a plural noun is invalid grammar.

To create tens, add numbers together using the conjunction sp (and) or hyphen -. For example: ein-sp-null or einnull = 10. The hyphen is optional but helps with clarity.

Correct examples
Ko yu ein PizzaI have one pizza.
Ko yu null PizzaI have zero pizza.
Ko yu twi PizzaI have two pizzas.
Ko yu ein-sp-null PizzaI have ten pizzas.
Incorrect
❌ Ko yu twi Pizzasnumber + plural is invalid

14Pluralization

To make a word plural, add the suffix s to the end of the word.

Standard pluralization
uport → uportsschool → schools
proakier → proakiersprogram → programs
kearpan → kearpansperson → persons

Exception: If a word ends in s, drop the final s and add es instead.

The suffix is added directly to the word without changing its original form otherwise.

15Questions

Put qa at the end of a sentence to turn it into a question.

When using question words like Wien, Wie, Ho, hau, Wai, Waien, do NOT use qa. The question word itself marks it as a question.

Examples
ptu ko yu uport amkalap.I don't have school tomorrow.
ptu ko yu uport amkalap qa?Don't I have school tomorrow?
Ko Wien nak uportWhen do I go to school? (question word, no qa needed)
Ko hau proakierHow do I program? (no qa needed with question word)

16Verb Conjugation

Verbs change based on the subject's person:

1st person singular (ko) = base verb form (no suffix)

All other subjects (su, mi, Po, qw, wq, it, etc.) = add im suffix

This marks when the action is not performed by a single person or represents multiple people/things.

Examples
Ko proakierI program (singular)
Su proakierimYou program (2nd person)
Mi proakierimWe program (1st person plural)
Po proakierimThey program (3rd person)
Ko kanI love (singular)
Su kanimYou love (non-1st-person-singular)

17Comparatives

To compare two things, use the structure: [Subject] + xy + [Adjective] + than + [comparison]

The verb xy (am/are) connects the subject to the adjective being compared.

Examples
Ko xy taop than suI am smart than you (I am smarter than you)
deth uport xyim tasful than deth problemThe school is useful than the problem
Mi xyim poat than Po xyim taopWe are dumb than they are smart

18Conditional Statements

Conditional statements use the structure: if [condition] del [statement]

This expresses "if [something], else [something else]."

Examples
if Ko xy taop del Ko xy poatIf I am smart, else I am dumb
if Su proakierim del Mi paltruder haponimIf you program, else my sister is hungry
if Su grtim pizza del Ko yu nullIf you got pizza, else I have zero

19Definite Articles

Use deth (the) before a noun to specify a particular person, place, or thing.

Examples
deth kearpanthe person
deth uportthe school
Ko nak deth uportI go to the school
deth mom pahthe mom dad (the parents)

20Adverbs

Adverbs modify verbs and describe how an action is performed. They go after the verb.

Examples
Ko proakier ticI program quickly
Su baltasim puticYou drink slowly
Mi parsafim kalipemWe understand easily
Ko nak ptukalpI go hardly (with difficulty)

21Colors and Color Modifiers

Colors are adjectives that describe nouns. Color modifiers (like lainzs for light and blainzs for dark) go before the color word.

Colors follow standard adjective placement rules (before the noun).

Examples
lainzs grenizsch uportlight green school
blainzs reipasfal chairdark yellow chair
Ko xy taop sp lainzs blauI am smart and light blue
deth blainzs pafstal kackirthe dark orange table

22Composing Larger Numbers

Larger numbers are composed by combining base numbers with multipliers: tien (10), hantirden (100), and thaiseond (1000).

Rules:

For tens: prefix the digit to tien (no spaces required, but hyphens allowed for clarity)

Tens
twitien20
threi-tien30

For compound numbers: combine with hyphens or directly

Compound
twi-tien-four24
threi-tien-noe39

For hundreds and thousands: use same pattern

Hundreds and thousands
ein-hantirden100
twi-hantirden-twitien-ein221
ein-thaiseond1000

Important: eintien is invalid; use just tien for 10 (not 1-10).

Examples in sentences
Ko yu twitien PizzaI have twenty pizzas
Su xyim threi-hantirden-ein suyrnYou are 301 years old
Mi nakim bi einthaiseond uportWe go in one thousand schools
